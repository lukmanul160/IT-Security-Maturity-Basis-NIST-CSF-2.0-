CYBERSECURITY GOVERNANCE

Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)

Kelola risiko keamanan siber dari vendor, supplier, partner, dan pihak ketiga dalam satu ruang kerja.

TPRM

RISK SCORING SYSTEM

Weighted TPRM risk summary

Total risk score = (Vendor Tier x 35%) + (CIA Risk Score x 40%) + (Related Risk Score x 25%).

35%Component A: Vendor TierTier 1 (High) = 100, Tier 2 (Medium) = 50, Tier 3 (Low) = 10.
40%Component B: CIA Risk ScoreUses the CIA score on a 0-100 scale. Lower CIA maturity scores produce higher risk contribution.
25%Component C: Related RiskVery Low = 1, Low = 2, Medium = 3, High = 4, Very High = 5.

RELATED RISK SUB-SCORE

Risk rating calculation

Sub-score = ((Very Low x 1) + (Low x 2) + (Medium x 3) + (High x 4) + (Very High x 5)) / (jumlah related risk x 5) x 100.

The summary is shown in color before the Actions column in the Third-party risk workspace.

Trusted partner governance

Framework ini mengatur vendor yang mengakses, memproses, atau mengembangkan sistem dengan data PII dan OT, termasuk software developer partners, SaaS, cloud, logistics, dan payroll providers.
